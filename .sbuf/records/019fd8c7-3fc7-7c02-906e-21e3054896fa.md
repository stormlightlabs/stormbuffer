Stormbuffer should position itself as human-reviewed, sourced, project-scoped memory that remains readable and repairable without the product. It should not try to match the hosted profiles, autonomous graph construction, broad connectors, or full agent-runtime scope offered by Supermemory, Zep, Cognee, and Letta unless user evidence shows those capabilities are necessary.
