Hybrid retrieval combines FTS and vector results with reciprocal-rank fusion using `1 / (60 + rank)` for each channel. Do not normalize or directly mix BM25 scores with vector distances because they are not comparable scales.

After fusion, only small deterministic adjustments are allowed: exact title or alias, current project scope, active status, checkpoint recency, and duplicate-chunk collapse. Never apply blanket recency decay to facts, decisions, or procedures; an old architectural decision does not become less relevant merely because it is old.
