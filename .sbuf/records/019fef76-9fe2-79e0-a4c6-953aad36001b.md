Stormbuffer should prioritize a five-minute path from installation to one cited retrieval. Cognee, Letta, Supermemory, and Mem0 all lead new users through short quickstarts or agent-first onboarding, while Stormbuffer currently asks users to understand installation, model setup, store scope, agent integration, and review policy before seeing the complete loop. The roadmap assigns this work to Milestone 5 without committing it to a v0.2 release.
